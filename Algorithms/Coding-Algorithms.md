# Coding Algorithms (DSA)

Data structures, algorithms, interview problem solving.
数据结构、算法与面试刷题。

---

## 1. Structured roadmaps / 结构化路线

### [jwasham/coding-interview-university](https://github.com/jwasham/coding-interview-university)

Complete CS study plan aimed at software-engineer interview readiness.
面向软件工程师面试就绪的完整 CS 学习计划。

**Value:** Single checklist for “what CS to cover” before company interviews.
**价值：** 入职前面试前「CS 要补什么」的一站式清单。

### [NeetCode](https://neetcode.io/)

Curated lists + videos; free tier enough to start.
精选题单 + 视频；免费档可起步。

**Value:** Best-known free solve-order for interviews.
**价值：** 面试刷题顺序最知名免费答案之一。

### [Striver A2Z DSA Sheet](https://takeuforward.org/strivers-a2z-dsa-course/strivers-a2z-dsa-course-sheet-2/)

Topic-complete sheet for multi-month prep.
多月备考的主题完整表。

### [labuladong/fucking-algorithm](https://github.com/labuladong/fucking-algorithm)

Pattern frameworks (two pointers, window, trees…), not one-off solutions.
模式框架，而非单题题解。

**Value:** Pattern recognition over memorization.
**价值：** 模式识别优于背题。
