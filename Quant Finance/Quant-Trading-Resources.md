# Quant Trading — Sites, Lists & Research Feeds

Non-repo resources prioritized for **systematic / quant trading** learning and research hygiene.
面向**系统化 / 量化交易**学习与研究卫生的非仓库资源（按用途分组）。

## 1. World models & market simulation / 世界模型与市场仿真

### [MicroWorld](https://microworldmarkets.com/)

World-model style view of finance: LLM-driven agents standing in for real participants, starting from visible on-chain positions (e.g. Hyperliquid) and cascading liquidations.
金融世界模型视角：用 LLM Agent 扮演真实参与者，从可见链上仓位（如 Hyperliquid）与连环强平出发。

Star Map and What-If engines replay shocks against real open interest; longer path aims at cross-venue risk infrastructure.
Star Map 与 What-If 引擎在真实持仓上重放冲击；更长期目标是跨场所风险基础设施。

Related open research line: [hongjin-he/MicroWorld](https://github.com/hongjin-he/MicroWorld).
相关开源研究线：[hongjin-he/MicroWorld](https://github.com/hongjin-he/MicroWorld)。

**Value:** Forces thinking about *interactive* risk (everyone’s forced selling), not single-book VaR.
**价值：** 逼着你从「交互风险」（所有人的被动卖出）思考，而非单本 VaR。

## 2. Curated lists & weekly research / 策展列表与周更研究

### [awesome-quant](https://github.com/wilsonfreitas/awesome-quant)

Large curated list of quant libraries, data, blogs, and tools across languages.
跨语言的量化库、数据、博客与工具大型策展列表。

**Value:** Single bookmark when you need “what exists” before deep dives.
**价值：** 深入前需要「有什么」时的单一书签。

### [ML-Quant](https://www.ml-quant.com/)

Weekly ranking of new arXiv / SSRN / RePEc quant papers with short takes and key figures.
每周筛选 arXiv / SSRN / RePEc 新量化论文，附短评与关键图。

**Value:** High-signal paper diet without drowning in preprints.
**价值：** 高信噪比论文饮食，避免被预印本淹没。

### [Papers With Backtest](https://paperswithbacktest.com)

Research papers paired with implementation / backtest-oriented material.
研究论文与实现 / 回测向材料配对。

**Value:** Bridges “read the paper” and “try a reproducible test”.
**价值：** 连接「读论文」与「可复现试跑」。

## 3. Practical education & platforms / 实践教育与平台

### [QuantStart](https://www.quantstart.com)

Long-running articles on algo trading, backtesting pitfalls, and quant career paths.
长期连载的算法交易、回测陷阱与量化职业路径文章。

**Value:** Still one of the clearest free written paths for systematic trading craft.
**价值：** 仍是系统化交易工艺最清晰的免费文字路径之一。

### [QuantInsti — free algo trading ebook (2026 ed.)](https://www.quantinsti.com/algo-trading-ebook)

Introductory map of algo/quant trading updated for AI/ML and current market structure.
算法/量化交易入门地图，已按 AI/ML 与当前市场结构更新。

**Value:** Structured overview before specialized engines in this library.
**价值：** 在进入本库专业引擎之前的结构化总览。

### [QuantConnect](https://www.quantconnect.com/)

Research + backtest + live path with free tier and Boot Camp; engine open as Lean.
研究 + 回测 + 实盘路径，有免费档与 Boot Camp；引擎开源为 Lean。

**Value:** Fastest way to ship a first honest backtest without building infra.
**价值：** 不自建基础设施也能最快做出第一个诚实回测。

### [Quantiacs](https://quantiacs.com/)

Strategy development platform with contests and allocation programs (futures-oriented history).
策略开发平台，含竞赛与资金分配计划（期货向历史较强）。

**Value:** Competition-style feedback loop on systematic strategies.
**价值：** 系统化策略的竞赛式反馈环。
